┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ WHY THIS WINS                                                               │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

1. ADDRESSES CORE FEAR
   "What will this actually DO?" is the #1 barrier to exploration.
   Preview removes uncertainty.

2. NON-DESTRUCTIVE EXPLORATION
   Users can try different templates without committing.
   Safe experimentation = more exploration.

3. EDUCATIONAL VALUE
   Users learn what teams do by reading previews.
   System becomes transparent, not a black box.

4. FOUNDATION FOR FUTURE
   Gallery, scenarios, and conversational UI all build on preview.
   This is the cornerstone.

5. IMMEDIATE VALUE
   Ships in 1-2 days. Users get confidence boost immediately.
   Fast ROI.
